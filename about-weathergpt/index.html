ABOUT THE PROJECT

About WeatherGPT

WeatherGPT is an educational weather information website created as part of a Search Engine Optimization mini-project.

Our objective

The project explores how a topic-focused website can organize useful weather content, target relevant search queries and apply technical and on-page SEO practices.

What you can explore

  • Weather forecast information and planning guidance.
  • Location-focused content such as Delhi weather.
  • Weather topics and seasonal information across India.
  • Educational articles about interpreting forecasts.

Data and limitations

The city lookup retrieves current conditions from Open-Meteo. Weather conditions change. WeatherGPT is not a substitute for official alerts or emergency instructions.
