Weather Forecast

A weather forecast helps you prepare for expected atmospheric conditions over a future period. Use forecasts as planning guidance and check updates as conditions change.

Search a city to view current conditions.
Live conditions use the Open-Meteo public API. Check official sources for alerts.

What to check in a forecast

  • Temperature: expected highs and lows.
  • Precipitation: chance and expected amount of rain.
  • Wind: speed and direction for outdoor plans.
  • Warnings: official alerts for hazardous conditions.
